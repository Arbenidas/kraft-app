# Code of Conduct

We want KRAFT to be a welcoming and constructive project for everyone.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and focus discussion on the work. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing and unwanted sexual attention are not acceptable.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately through the security contact process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be handled with discretion and appropriate action may include a warning, removal of content, or removal from project spaces.
